Timeline

Key Moments in Intel’s Sustainability Story

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel, creating a company that would define modern computing.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel unveils the 4004, the first commercial microprocessor, accelerating the personal computing revolution.

1978

8086 Processor

The 8086 establishes the x86 architecture, shaping the future of PCs, servers, and enterprise computing.

1985

386 Processor

The 386 introduces 32-bit performance and multitasking, unlocking a new era of productivity and innovation.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel records its highest annual greenhouse gas emissions before investing in cleaner manufacturing and renewable energy systems.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches the RISE strategy, reinforcing its commitments to sustainable, inclusive, and responsible innovation.
